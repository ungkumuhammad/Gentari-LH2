Notes & sources

  1. SEC ≈ 8–9 kWh/kg, varies with liquefier scale/boundary conditions — KHI reply Q1 (kawasaki-2026-questionnaire).
  2. Precooling: N₂ precooling proven in service; hydrocarbon mixed refrigerant under consideration for the future — reply Q5/Q13.
  3. Ortho–para conversion inside the liquefier, Fe-based catalyst in service (Co-based under evaluation); no expected degradation — reply Q6/Q7.
  4. Liquefaction cycle: H₂ Claude cycle with N₂ precooling, proven track record — reply Q13.
  5. Detailed PFD / heat & mass balance not disclosed by KHI — reply Q10: shareable only "at Feasibility Study timing." Sub-steps shown are the process elements KHI did disclose, not a full engineering PFD. GAP
  6. BOG downstream of the liquefaction plant is re-liquefied — via an ejector in the liquefier or a compressor with cryogenic-rated suction — reply Q3/Q14.
  7. Export/loading terminal: 64,000 m³/tank, ×3 at Base scale (2.5B Nm³/y) / ×4 at Large scale (10B Nm³/y); BOR 0.1%/day at rest — kawasaki-2026-supplemental.
  8. Loading arm: 6-inch arm demonstrated at TRL 6–7 on the Suiso Frontier pilot; 16-inch commercial-scale arm not yet verified (~3 years out); both by TBG — reply Q19.
  9. Commercial-scale target vessel: 160,000 m³ @ 29.6 km/h (~16 kn) — kawasaki-2026-supplemental. A 40,000 m³ vessel is under construction using Suiso Frontier's proven tank technology (reply Q29); the demo vessel Suiso Frontier itself is 1,250 m³ (kawasaki-hydrogen-activities).
  10. No cargo-tank filling-ratio restriction; no sloshing impact on BOG — reply Q20.
  11. Voyage boil-off rate is not disclosed by KHI as a standalone figure — reply Q23 states only that fuel-gas consumption equals the BOR, without giving a %/day value. GAP
  12. BOG-as-fuel: KHI is developing a dual-fuel (DF) engine that burns cargo BOG as propulsion fuel — reply Q23/Q32.
  13. Excess BOG (when sailing on MGO only and tank pressure exceeds MARVS) is burned via the onboard gas combustion unit (GCU) — reply Q26.
  14. Onboard reliquefaction is not adopted — too much electrical energy and onboard space required — reply Q33.
  15. Maximum sailing distance is limited only by the MGO fuel tank, not by BOG — reply Q21.
  16. Import/receiving terminal: 65,000 m³/tank, ×3 at Base scale / ×11 at Large scale; BOR 0.1%/day at rest — kawasaki-2026-supplemental.
  17. Import-side BOG handling is shown as re-liquefied by analogy with the export terminal — KHI's reply on reliquefaction (Q3/Q14) specifically addresses the export/loading side; treat the import-side treatment as an assumption, not a KHI-confirmed fact. ASSUMPTION
  18. Vaporizer: Open-Rack Vaporizer (ORV) using seawater as the heat source; a dedicated heat-source system if nearby aquaculture constrains cold-seawater discharge — reply Q17.
  19. Regasification heat duty ≈ 3.8 MJ/kg-LH₂ — reply Q18.
  20. LH₂ normal boiling point ≈ 20.3 K / ≈ 1 bar(a) — data/properties/lh2-properties.csv, tagged needs-source pending a formal NIST/CODATA citation (see docs/memory.md open item).
  21. Feed condition (30 barg / 30°C) is a Gentari project-specified boundary condition for this diagram, not a figure disclosed by KHI. ASSUMPTION
  22. Delivery (send-out) pressure at the regas outlet has not been specified by KHI or by the project yet. ASSUMPTION until specified

System boundary: gate-to-gate, electrolyzer GH₂ battery limit → regasification GH₂ send-out. Production (electrolysis) and downstream distribution/end-use are out of scope.

Cardinal rule applied: every block/stream label is either cited to a KHI/IAE source or flagged as an assumption/gap — nothing on this diagram is an invented process detail (see CLAUDE.md §4).
