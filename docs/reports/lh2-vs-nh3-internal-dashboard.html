The mock-up plots 2026–2045. We hold no dated cost path, so the headline is by scale case (Base, Large, Tech) from the IAE-2019 stack that KHI supplied.

How to read it

  • At Base scale LH₂ is 1.3 JPY/Nm³ above NH₃ (37.6 vs 36.3). At Large scale it is 4.1 below (30.7 vs 34.8).
  • NH₃'s large cracking step (8.6–8.9) is the cost LH₂ avoids; LH₂'s terminals (5.6 loading and 6.3 receiving at Base) are the cost it carries.
  • The sign change sits between 225 and 900 kt/y. With two scale points we cannot locate it.

        Caveats to resolve (from our own data)

        • Energy basis conflict. KHI's supplemental slide gives liquefier power 0.55 kWh/Nm³ (= 6.1 kWh/kg H₂ at 0.0899 kg/Nm³, density citation pending). KHI's questionnaire gives 8–9 kWh/kg today. The IAE stack appears to use the lower figure, so it is not a "today" cost.
        • Not like-for-like carriers. The IAE basis uses 160,000 m³ LH₂ ships against 38,000 m³ NH₃ ships.
        • Vendor supplied. The IAE stack reached us through a KHI presentation (May 2026) of a 2019 workshop; the primary document is not in the repository, and the unit (JPY/Nm³) is inferred.
        • Database remarks open. KBR's SEC cell holds the electrical figure (0.290), so KBR SEC is GAP here; Casale's SEC 8.18 and 0.04 kWh/kg are unverified in the vendor package; the Hanwha Impact plant is listed as "Operational" while its COD is unconfirmed.
