### DNV — July 2026 edition of class rules & standards (incl. gas-fuelled hydrogen)
- **What:** DNV released the **July 2026 edition** of its rules and standards for
  the classification of ships and offshore units — **92 documents** published —
  including a **rules concept for gas-fuelled hydrogen amended to fit the IMO
  MSC interim guideline** safety concept.
- **In force:** **1 January 2027**.
- **Why it matters:** class-rule maturation is a gating item for LH2 carriers and
  H2-fuelled ships; the alignment to the MSC interim guideline signals the

## 5. Publications & research

### Review: advances & challenges in cryogenic LH2 maritime transportation
- **What:** a review paper in *Renewable & Sustainable Energy Reviews* — "Advances
  and challenges in cryogenic liquefied hydrogen (LH2) maritime transportation:
  thermal performance, tank design, boil-off gas management, and techno-economic
  considerations." Reports LH2 as having the **highest boil-off among major H2
  carriers at ~3.44 %/day** (and transport cost ~3.74 $/GJ).
- **Date:** **published online 28 Feb 2026**; RSER **Vol. 233, Jun 2026**, art.
  116850; **DOI 10.1016/j.rser.2026.116850**. → **NOT within the 14–21 Jul digest
  week** — included as adjacent literature, date now confirmed via indexing.
- **Why it matters — repo relevance:** this repo's scenario model has a **known
  open gap: KHI never disclosed a standalone voyage boil-off rate** (only that
  fuel-gas consumption = BOR, and that at-rest terminal BOR ≈ 0.1 %/day). A
  peer-reviewed ~3.44 %/day voyage figure is a candidate external benchmark to
  bound that gap — see Promotion candidates.
- **Figure:** LH2 voyage BOG **≈ 3.44 %/day** — cited; note this is a *literature
  review* value, tank/voyage basis not yet read from full text (abstract only;
  full text paywalled, not fetched this run).
- **Source:** [doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2026.116850](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2026.116850)
  (ScienceDirect; abstract free, full text paywalled) · tier: `journal` ·
  accessed 2026-07-21. Open-access companion (different figures):
  [JMSE 10.3390/jmse13091748](https://doi.org/10.3390/jmse13091748).
